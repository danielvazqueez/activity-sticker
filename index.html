Activity Sticker Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Activity Sticker lets you turn your workouts into shareable images. This policy explains what the app accesses and what happens to that information. The short version: everything stays on your device. We do not collect, store or share any of your data.

Information the app accesses

Health and workout data. With your permission, the app reads workouts from Apple Health, such as distance, duration, pace, heart rate and the workout route. This data is read only to draw your sticker or image. It is never sent off your device.

Location from workout routes. Route maps come from the GPS route saved with your workout in Apple Health. The app does not track your location on its own.

Photos and camera. If you choose a background photo or save an image, the app accesses only the photos you pick and saves only the images you create.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no advertising. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information, health data or usage data.

Apple Health data

Health data read through Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with third parties and never stored in iCloud by the app. You can change or remove the app's access at any time in the Health app or in Settings, under Privacy and Security, then Health.

Sharing

Images you create stay on your device unless you choose to share them, for example to Instagram or Messages. Anything you share is handled by the app or service you share it with, under their own privacy policies.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
