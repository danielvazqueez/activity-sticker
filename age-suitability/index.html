Activity Sticker: age suitability

Published by LuCa Labs. Last updated November 22, 2026.

4+
Suitable for everyone, ages 4 and up.
The app turns your own workouts into an image you can save or share. It has no chat, no ads, no purchases and no content from other people.

What the app does

Activity Sticker reads workouts you have already recorded in Apple Health, for example a run or ride from your iPhone, Apple Watch, Garmin or another GPS tracker. It draws your route and adds your distance, pace and time over a photo you choose, so you can make a sticker or image of your activity. You decide whether to save the image or share it, using the standard iOS share options.

What the app does not have

  • No chat, messaging or contact with other users.
  • No user generated content shown to other people inside the app.
  • No social feed, public profiles or follower lists.
  • No advertising.
  • No in-app purchases, subscriptions or loot boxes.
  • No web browsing inside the app.
  • No violence, mature themes, gambling or contests.
  • No medical advice, diagnosis or treatment guidance.

Data the app uses

With your permission, the app reads workouts and workout routes from Apple Health, and photos you pick from your library. It does not write to Apple Health. Everything happens on your device. Your workouts, routes and photos are not sent to us or to anyone else, and the app has no accounts, analytics or servers. The app does not show your location to other users, and nothing is shared unless you choose to share an image yourself. You can turn off Health or Photos access at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Guidance for parents

The app is suitable for all ages. Because an exported image can show a route map, it can reveal where an activity started or ended, such as a home or school. We suggest parents talk with children about which images are safe to post publicly. Health and Photos access can be managed in Settings, and Screen Time lets you limit or approve app use.
